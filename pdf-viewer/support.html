Getting started

Tap Open PDF to pick a document from Files or iCloud Drive. The app imports its own copy, so your original file is never changed. Your library remembers every document and reopens it on the page where you left off.

Editing a document

Open a document and use the toolbar to highlight or underline text, draw with freehand ink, add text boxes and sticky notes, fill form fields, and stamp your saved signature. From the page grid you can reorder pages by dragging, rotate pages, or delete them, and the library's merge mode combines two or more PDFs into one new document. Edits are saved to the app's copy; share the document to export it anywhere.

Ads and Remove Ads

The app is free and shows one small banner plus one full-screen ad after about every five minutes of active reading. The one-time Remove Ads purchase in Settings turns all ads off forever — it is not a subscription. If you bought it on another device, use Restore Purchases in Settings.

Your documents stay yours

Documents, signatures, and library data are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for details.
