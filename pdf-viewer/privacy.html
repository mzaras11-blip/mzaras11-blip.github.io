Privacy Policy — PDF Viewer & Editor

Effective November 16, 2026

PDF Viewer & Editor ("the app") is developed by Michael Aras. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your documents

PDFs you open are copied into the app's private storage on your device so the app can edit them. Your documents, annotations, signatures, and library data are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any server, and we have no access to them. The app runs no analytics of its own and requires no account.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob: one small banner, and one full-screen ad after about every five minutes of active reading. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, and ad interaction data, as described in Google's policy for partner sites.

On first launch the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you will still see ads — they just won't be personalized. A one-time Remove Ads in-app purchase disables all ads; once purchased, the app does not load ads.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is a general-purpose document tool and is not directed at children.
