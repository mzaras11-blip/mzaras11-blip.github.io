About S.W.I.S.H – Crossword Battle

  • One shared board, real battles: challenge friends and take turns building on the same crossword.
  • 5 tiles, 60 seconds a turn — every move counts, and the clock keeps it honest.
  • Split Screen duels: two players on one phone, face to face.
  • Daily Puzzle with streaks, plus a shareable result card when you win.
  • Rush mode: a real-time 3-minute race where combos multiply your score.
  • Free to play, with an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase for $4.99. Play as a guest or sign in to keep your matches everywhere.
