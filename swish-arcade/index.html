About S.W.I.S.H - Crossword ARCADE

  • The S.W.I.S.H crossword battle rebuilt in a cartoon arcade look — chunky tiles, a gold-framed board and a mascot who follows you through the game.
  • Avatars with emotions: your face and your rival's react live — happy on a score, excited on a lead, worried when you fall behind.
  • Rush races: a real-time 3-minute race against the CPU or against a friend online, with combo multipliers up to ×5.
  • Picture clues on the grid, Split Screen duels on one phone, and a fresh Daily Challenge every day.
  • Background music and sound effects, an in-game store, and an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase.
