About SWISH CITY

  • Endless running: sprint through a glossy golden city that keeps getting faster.
  • Play as Swisher — the blue mascot from S.W.I.S.H, red cap and all.
  • Collect letter tiles scattered along the run while you dodge traffic and obstacles.
  • Districts roll from day into neon night as your run goes on.
  • Free to play — chase a new best distance every run.
